Stacked layers

Feed-forward (MLP)

Dense layers in a stack. Every unit in one layer connects to every unit in the next, and data moves one way, input to output.

Inside one unit. Each input is multiplied by its own weight, the products are summed with a bias, and the sum goes through a nonlinearity. A layer is many of these units side by side.

Reuse across space

Convolutional (CNN)

A small kernel slides over the grid and writes one feature map per kernel. Pooling shrinks the maps, stacked convolutions see wider areas, and a dense head scores the result.

Reuse across time

Recurrent (RNN)

One cell reads the sequence a step at a time, taking the step's input and its own previous state. The three boxes are one cell drawn once per step; a per-step output can also be read from each hₜ.

LSTM

An RNN whose cell carries a second state, cₜ, changed only by a forget-gate multiply and an input-gate add. That path lets information and gradients survive over many more steps than a plain RNN.

Reuse across tokens, all at once

Transformer

Every token attends to every other in one step, rather than one after another as in an RNN, so the order comes only from the position vectors. The encoder reads the input; the decoder writes the output a token at a time, attending to the encoder and to what it has written so far.

BERT and Sentence-BERT

BERT is the Transformer's encoder half alone, pretrained on large text by filling in masked words. BERT-base stacks 12 encoder blocks; all-MiniLM-L6-v2, the Sentence-BERT model this repo runs by default, stacks 6. BERT also takes a pair of segments, [CLS] A [SEP] B [SEP], which is how a cross-encoder scores two names together. Sentence-BERT averages BERT's per-token vectors into one vector per sentence, and is fine-tuned so similar sentences land close together.

Reuse across inputs

Siamese

Not a layer type but a wiring: two subnetworks of the same architecture, sharing their weights, each take one side of a pair. The subnetwork can be any of the networks above. Twins of the same architecture that do not share weights are usually called pseudo-Siamese.

Siamese losses

The twins are the same under every loss; what changes is what goes in and what the loss asks of the distance. For blocking the difference that counts is the bottom row: whether u itself was trained to be searched by cosine.
